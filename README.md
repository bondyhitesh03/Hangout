Introducing Hangout

Hangout is a social media platform designed to bring people together through entertainment, information, and meaningful interactions. Our goal was to create an all-in-one platform where users can stay updated with current events, share their creativity, connect with others, and have fun—all in one place.

Developing Hangout has been an exciting journey filled with learning, experimentation, and problem-solving. While we're proud of what we've built, we're also committed to being transparent about the challenges we're still working to overcome.

Features of Hangout
🏠 Home Page

The home page is designed to keep users engaged with a variety of dynamic content.

😂 Randomized Jokes

Every visit brings a fresh collection of randomized jokes to brighten your day and keep the experience entertaining.

📰 Latest News

Stay informed with the latest news across multiple fields, ensuring users have access to current events from a wide range of topics.

📸 Snippet Section

The Snippet section allows users to explore short-form content, including photos and videos, making content discovery quick and enjoyable.

📝 Create and Share Posts

Users can express themselves by posting:

Photos
Videos
Blogs

This enables creators to share experiences, ideas, and stories with the Hangout community.

🎯 Trivia Section

Our interactive trivia feature lets users answer simple questions to earn Aura Points, adding a fun, gamified experience that encourages learning and engagement.

💬 Community Chat

Hangout includes a global community chat where users can communicate with everyone on the platform, exchange ideas, and participate in discussions.

🔒 Private Chats

For more personal conversations, users can connect through private messaging with friends and other users.

Challenges and Current Bugs

Like every growing application, Hangout still has a few issues that we're actively working to resolve. We believe transparency helps build trust with our users.

1. Website Performance

Currently, Hangout experiences slower loading times because it relies on free-tier services and hosting. These services have limitations on computing resources and request handling.

Our Effort:
We are optimizing backend operations, reducing unnecessary API calls, and exploring more efficient hosting solutions to improve overall performance.

2. Profile Doesn't Appear Immediately After Registration

After creating a new account, the profile page may not appear instantly. Users currently need to refresh the page before the profile becomes visible.

Our Effort:
We're improving state management and authentication synchronization so newly created profiles load automatically without requiring a manual refresh.

3. Posts and Community Messages Don't Update Instantly

When users create a community message or publish a post, it doesn't appear immediately. A page refresh is currently required to view the latest content.

Our Effort:
We're working on implementing real-time updates using technologies such as WebSockets or real-time database listeners so content appears instantly without refreshing.

4. Trivia Questions Reveal Upcoming Answers

We've identified an issue in the Trivia section where the answer to the next question is unintentionally revealed.

Our Effort:
We're restructuring the trivia logic to ensure each question remains independent and that answers are only displayed after the user submits a response.

What We've Learned

Building Hangout has been much more than writing code. It has involved learning about user experience, backend optimization, real-time communication, authentication, content management, and debugging complex interactions between different components.

Every bug we've encountered has helped us better understand how to build a more reliable and scalable platform. Rather than viewing these issues as setbacks, we see them as opportunities to improve the experience for every user.

Looking Ahead

Our vision for Hangout extends beyond its current features. We plan to continue improving performance, fixing existing bugs, and introducing exciting new capabilities based on user feedback.

Some of our future goals include:

Faster loading times
Real-time updates for posts and chats
Improved profile management
Enhanced trivia gameplay
Better scalability as the user base grows
More interactive social features
Conclusion

Hangout is still evolving, and every update brings us closer to creating a faster, smoother, and more engaging social media experience. We appreciate everyone who supports the platform, reports bugs, and shares feedback.

Thank you for being part of our journey. We're excited about what's coming next and look forward to making Hangout better with every release.
